Level 2¶

Complete closure of all \(\binom{6}{2} = 15\) pairs from \(L_1\) — 203 points, 10 lines, 16 circles, and 50 golden sections (34 on explicit lines).

Overview¶

Every pair of the 6 points in \(L_1\) is processed, yielding 10 unique lines, 16 unique circles, and 203 unique points across \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}, \sqrt{7}, \ldots)\). Within \(L_2\), 50 golden sections emerge, 34 of which lie directly on the 10 constructed lines, forming 7 golden chains (up to length 4) across 8 distinct scales.

Golden Section Ancestor Explorer¶

Step through or play all 34 explicit-line golden sections in Level 2. Each frame isolates the minimal Euclidean ancestor tree back to \(A\) and \(B\):

  • Cyan lines: ancestor lines of the 3 golden section points.

  • Magenta circles: ancestor circles of the 3 golden section points.

  • Gold / amber segments: the highlighted golden section \((P, Q, R)\).

Use Scroll to zoom, Drag to pan, or Prev / Play / Next (or arrow keys / spacebar) to inspect each construction.
