Levels¶

An iterative constructive closure of the Euclidean plane starting from two given points \(A\) and \(B\).

What Are Levels?¶

In classical Euclidean geometry, every straightedge-and-compass construction begins from two seed points separated by unit distance:

\[L_0 = \{A, B\}, \qquad A = \left(-\frac{1}{2}, 0\right), \quad B = \left(\frac{1}{2}, 0\right).\]

Given any set of constructed points \(L_n\) at level \(n\), the next level \(L_{n+1}\) is generated by taking every unordered pair of distinct points \(\{P, Q\} \subset L_n\) and applying the three fundamental Euclidean operations:

  1. Line \([P\,Q]\) — the straight line passing through \(P\) and \(Q\).

  2. Circle \((P\,Q)\) — the circle centered at \(P\) passing through \(Q\).

  3. Circle \((Q\,P)\) — the circle centered at \(Q\) passing through \(P\).

Each newly added line or circle is intersected against all existing structures in the model. Every intersection point is deduplicated symbolically in exact algebraic coordinates and added to the point set, yielding the monotonic hierarchy:

\[L_0 \subset L_1 \subset L_2 \subset L_3 \subset \cdots\]

Recursive Ancestors¶

Every constructed point \(X\) in \(L_n\) (other than the seeds \(A\) and \(B\)) is born from the intersection of two parent structures (lines or circles). Each parent line or circle, in turn, was constructed from two parent points. Tracing this directed acyclic graph backward until reaching \(A\) and \(B\) isolates the minimal Euclidean proof tree for any point or golden section \((P, Q, R)\) with \(PQ / QR = \phi\).

  • Cyan lines: minimal ancestor lines of the golden section points.

  • Magenta circles: minimal ancestor circles of the golden section points.

  • Gold / amber segments: the golden section \((P, Q, R)\) highlighted in place.

Levels Catalog¶

Level 1

The vesica piscis closure from the single seed pair \(\{A, B\}\) — 6 points, 1 line, 2 circles, and 0 golden sections.

Level 2

Complete closure of all \(\binom{6}{2} = 15\) pairs from \(L_1\) — 203 points, 10 lines, 16 circles, and 50 golden sections (34 on explicit lines).

Level 3

Frontier exploration across \(\binom{203}{2} = 20,503\) pairs — 17,562 unique lines, 32,719 unique circles, and ~2.37 billion intersection slots.
