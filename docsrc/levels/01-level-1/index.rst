Level 1

.. container:: lead

   The *vesica piscis* closure from the single seed pair :math:`\{A, B\}` — 6 points, 1 line, 2 circles, and 0 golden sections.

Construction
============

Starting from :math:`L_0 = \{A, B\}`, there is only :math:`\binom{2}{2} = 1` unordered pair :math:`\{A, B\}`. Applying the three operations produces:

- **1 line**: :math:`[A\,B]`
- **2 circles**: :math:`(A\,B)` and :math:`(B\,A)`

Their intersections yield **4 new points** (two along the line :math:`[A\,B]` at :math:`(-3/2, 0)` and :math:`(3/2, 0)`, and the two equilateral apexes at :math:`(0, \pm \sqrt{3}/2)`), bringing :math:`L_1` to **6 points** total. No golden sections appear yet at Level 1.
