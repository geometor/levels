About This Edition
------------------

Exhaustive compass-and-straightedge level constructions and emergent golden sections.

``geometor.levels`` is developed and maintained by **phiarchitect** as part of the **GEOMETOR** organization.


The GEOMETOR Mission
--------------------

The GEOMETOR project, directed by **phi ARCHITECT**, is dedicated to the systematic recovery, exact formalization, and dynamic re-expression of foundational geometric and philosophical knowledge:

- **Extracting Historical Semantic Knowledge**: Recovering the deductive structure of classical geometric, axiomatic, and philosophical works—elevating verbatim primary prose, primary-source folio scans (``publish folio``), and calibrated ``.euclid-graphic`` diagrams into addressable semantic graphs.
- **Exact Symbolic Grounding (``geometor.model``)**: Anchoring geometric constructions in 100% exact symbolic algebra (``SymPy``) and Rational Trigonometry invariants over closed-form radical fields.
- **Repurposing Knowledge Across Mediums**: Deploying extracted structures across multi-track **Digital Editions**, EPUB 3 volumes, computable canons, interactive SVG laboratories, and visual broadcasts.
